# PyBlastRadius - Use Cases

## Overview

**Operational Blast Radius Intelligence Platform** — Predict cascading failures and quantify business impact across infrastructure, data pipelines, and AI systems. > **Not to be confused with [PyDBTGuard](https://github.com/Mullassery/PyDBTGuard), [PyBeamGuard](https://github.com/Mullassery/PyBeamGuard), or [PyAirflowTester](https://github.com/Mullassery/PyAirflowTester):** those validate a single pipeline technology; PyBlastRadius simulates cascading-failure blast radius across infrastructure, data pipelines, and AI systems.
